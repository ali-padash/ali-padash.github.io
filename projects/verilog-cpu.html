Course project

8-bit Processor in Verilog

A small accumulator-based CPU with a hard-wired control unit, designed register by register and simulated in ModelSim.

When
Spring 2023
Course
Computer Architecture
Course grade
19.45 / 20
Team
Team of two
  • Verilog
  • ModelSim
  • Digital design

Overview

Designing a working processor from the registers up, in the style of Mano's basic computer.

What I did

  • Registers AR and PC (5-bit) and DR, AC, IR, TR and OUTR (8-bit), connected over a shared bus with a select line.
  • 8-bit instructions: a 3-bit opcode and a 5-bit address into a 32-byte memory; the program is stored in ROM.
  • Hard-wired control: a sequence counter and decoders produce timing signals T0–T5 and opcode lines D0–D7, and gate logic turns them into register load, clear and increment signals.
  • ALU operations: add, subtract, signed compare (−1 / 0 / 1), shift left, complement, load, and an integer square root.
  • Verified in ModelSim by printing the memory contents after each instruction.
