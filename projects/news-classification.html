Course project

Persian News Topic Classification

Classifying about 12,500 Persian news articles into six topics with an n-gram voting ensemble and a TF-IDF neural network.

When
Spring 2024
Course
Artificial Intelligence and Expert Systems
Course grade
19.89 / 20
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • Keras
  • Hazm
  • Persian NLP

Overview

News sites tag their articles with about 100 fine-grained labels. The task: predict an article's topic from its title and text.

What I did

  • Mapped the roughly 100 site tags onto six topics: social, economy, provinces, international, politics, and culture / sport / science.
  • Cleaned Persian text with Hazm (normalisation, tokenisation, stemming, stop-word removal) and stripped HTML entities, links and digits. Titles were given extra weight by repeating them before the body.
  • Soft-voting ensemble of SVM, XGBoost and Logistic Regression on 1–3-gram counts.
  • A second model: TF-IDF features (7,500 terms, 1–3-grams) feeding a small Keras MLP with dropout and early stopping.

Results

  • On a held-out 20% split (2,083 articles): 84% accuracy and macro F1 0.82 for the voting ensemble.
  • 85.1% accuracy (weighted F1 0.846) for the TF-IDF + MLP model.
  • Per-topic F1 ranged from 0.73 (culture, sport and science) to 0.88 (international).
