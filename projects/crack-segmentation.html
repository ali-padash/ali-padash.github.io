Course project

Asphalt Crack Segmentation

Pixel-level crack detection with a U-Net built from scratch in PyTorch, trained on a class-collected dataset of about 2,100 photos, 50 of them taken and labelled by me.

When
Spring 2025
Course

  • Python

Overview

Finding thin cracks in road surfaces, pixel by pixel: for every pixel, decide whether it belongs to a crack.

What I did

  • Photographed 40 cracked and 10 undamaged asphalt surfaces and labelled the cracks as polygons in Roboflow, as my share of a dataset the whole class collected (about 2,100 photos).
  • Turned the pooled COCO-format dataset into binary masks and re-split it 70 / 15 / 15 into train, validation and test sets, keeping the share of crack-free images equal in each.
  • Built a U-Net-style network in PyTorch from scratch: four encoder levels, a bottleneck and four decoder levels joined by skip connections.
  • Trained it for 20 epochs with a combined cross-entropy and Dice loss, and wrote an evaluation that reports IoU and draws the predicted cracks over the photos.
  • Wrote augmentation that rotates and scales each photo together with its crack outlines, and a dropout-regularised version of the network.
