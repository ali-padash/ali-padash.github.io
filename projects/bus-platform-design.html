Bus Ticketing Platform: Analysis and Design

A full UML design and a clickable Figma prototype for an intercity bus-ticket platform, the same domain I later built in Rahi.

Overview

Analysing and designing a platform where passengers buy intercity bus tickets, bus companies list their trips, and administrators oversee both.

What I did

  • BPMN business process, use-case, class, sequence and state diagrams for passengers, bus companies and administrators.
  • A class model covering users, companies, buses, journeys, tickets, payments, reviews, discounts and a cargo service, plus external services for OTP login, payments and notifications.
  • A clickable Figma prototype of the passenger flow: login, trip search, results, seat selection, passenger details, payment and ticket download.
